## GovCon Intelligence Agent: first public release
Local-first federal opportunity intelligence. Free public sources (SAM.gov with your own key, USAspending, GSA forecast, GAO), new/changed and recompete detection, six-factor scoring with explanations, 7-sheet Excel report, natural-language Q&A (optional AI), local web UI, SQLite, guided setup, demo mode.
Not affiliated with the U.S. Government. macOS tested; Linux/Windows/Docker experimental.
